Sunday's PIK stop is tighter than the others with only few hours of buffer before you need to leave for CGK, so keep that shopping and brunch run efficient. If you want to shop at Alchemist or Saff & Co, check their opening hours and plan accordingly. If you want to have brunch at Tuan Mudo, check their opening hours too.
